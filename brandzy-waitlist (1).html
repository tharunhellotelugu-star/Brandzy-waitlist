Questions

What is Brandzy?

A web app for creators that keeps your storefront, brand deals, invoices, client CRM and media kit behind one shareable link. It’s built for creators in India and open to creators anywhere.

Does Brandzy take a commission?

No. You pay a flat monthly fee. Fans and brands pay you directly.

How much will it cost?

Plans start at ₹999 a month in India, or US$12 a month outside India. Waitlist members get founding-member pricing.
